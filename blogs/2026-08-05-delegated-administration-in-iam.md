Why Delegated Administration Matters Now Identity has moved from a back-office IT function to the operating layer for digital business. Every login, approval, entitlement change, and exception request now affects revenue, service delivery, compliance exposure, and customer trust. When a partner cannot access a portal, a contractor keeps privileges after a project ends, or a machine identity is over-permissioned in production, the issue is not merely administrative friction.
